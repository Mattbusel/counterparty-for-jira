# Privacy policy: Counterparty for Jira

Last updated November 1, 2026. Publisher: Tensorust LLC, 404 Read Ave, Tuckahoe, NY 10707, USA. Contact: mattbusel@tensorust.com.

- **What the app stores.** For each check: the counterparty name that was screened, the verdict, the findings and links from public sources, and the time of the check. This is stored in your Atlassian site (Forge storage and a Jira work item property) and in comments you choose to post. Tensorust LLC runs no servers for this app and receives none of this data.
- **Where requests go.** To screen a name, the app sends that name to the public government and registry services listed in the app manifest (U.S. Treasury OFAC, HHS-OIG, GLEIF, SEC, USAspending.gov, openFDA, CFPB). Those services' own privacy terms apply.
- **Atlassian user data.** The app does not store Atlassian account ids, emails or profile data.
- **Deletion.** Uninstalling the app removes its Forge storage. Work item properties, labels and comments stay in Jira until you delete them.
